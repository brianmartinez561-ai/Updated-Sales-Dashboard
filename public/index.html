Targets

Enter the monthly target per employee. Card totals are multiplied by the number of employees selected and prorated to the selected dates. CSAT and P360 are goal averages. To load store targets from Excel, add a sheet named Targets with a Store column plus one column per metric (or Store, Metric, Target columns). Each store’s monthly total is divided evenly among that store’s employees. Without a Store column, the values are treated as per-employee targets.
